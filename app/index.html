    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    Four stages, in order. Stage 1 is the story (60–80 hours); Stage 2 the side questlines; Stage 3 the post-story cleanup and collections; Stage 4 the 100% checklist and the platinum. Nothing on the checklist is missable — it can all be done in free-roam after the story. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: there is no mission replay and no New Game+.

      Save discipline (read this first)

      • 15 manual save slots + 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start and one at the end of each chapter (10 slots), and rotate the other 5 for free-roam.
      • Never turn on a cheat during a trophy run: cheats disable trophies and saving.
      • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — the only trophy that may be missable.
      • Scan vehicles and study animals as you go: 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals count toward 100%.

      Missable watch

      At launch, exactly one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else in the list is confirmed safe: story trophies unlock automatically, and every side questline stays available in free-roam after the story. The watch below is built from the official descriptions plus the launch guides.

      Why the rest are safe

      • All 12 story trophies unlock automatically when their chapter or mission ends.
      • Quest givers who go quiet mid-story (Kenton, for example) come back later — all trophy-related characters are available again after the story.
      • Optional missions that disappear do not matter for trophies: there are far more than the 100 the checklist asks for.
      • A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later close, but they also spawn elsewhere and are not needed for any trophy.
